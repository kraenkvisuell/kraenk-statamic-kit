{{# Embedded video (project page, blog "video" set) behind a consent gate. The `videoConsent` component (resources/js/video-consent.js) shows the notice and the accept button from site_settings (video_consent_text/_button) until third-party videos are accepted (localStorage); then the `videoPlayer` component (resources/js/video-player.js) inserts the Vimeo/YouTube iframe. The privacy link opens the footer's datenschutz overlay (partials/footer). Deliberately client-side: the HTML is the same for every visitor, so the page stays static-cacheable. Fills the width at the video's ratio (default 16:9). Params: key (Vimeo/YouTube URL), ratio ("4:3", optional), autoplay (optional), classes (extra classes for the wrapper, e.g. margins / max height). #}}
